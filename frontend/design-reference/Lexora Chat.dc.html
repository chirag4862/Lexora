Punishment for murder under BNS?
What is the punishment for murder under the new law?
§
LEXORA
Under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, murder is dealt with in Section 103 — it replaced Section 302 of the IPC.

Whoever commits murder shall be punished with death or imprisonment for life, and shall also be liable to a fine. If the murder is committed by a group of five or more persons on specified grounds, each member faces the same punishment under Section 103(2).

The procedure for the trial follows the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita — the case is triable exclusively by a Court of Session.
